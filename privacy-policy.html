Privacy Policy

MeetApp is a mobile application that accesses contacts to enhance user experience by providing features such as contact-based event invitations, recommendations, or other relevant functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Contacts

With your explicit permission, MeetApp accesses the contact names and phone numbers stored on your device. Phone numbers from your address book are transmitted securely to our servers so we can:

  • Identify which of your contacts already use MeetApp.
  • Let you invite friends to events.
  • Personalize your app experience.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contacts, and we do not share them with third-party advertisers. Contacts are used solely to provide the features described above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your contacts within the app.
  • To enable social features like inviting or identifying friends.
  • To improve app performance and usability.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or contacts. We may share information only:

  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To prevent fraud or protect our rights.
  • With your explicit consent.

4. Data Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure.

5. Where We Store Your Data & International Transfers

MeetApp is operated using Amazon Web Services (AWS), and authentication is provided by Google Firebase. Your data is currently stored and processed on AWS infrastructure located in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai, India) region.

If you access MeetApp from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another region outside India, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in India, which may not offer the same level of data protection as your home country. Where such transfers are subject to data-protection law, they are carried out under appropriate safeguards, including the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, as provided under our data-processing agreement with AWS.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account and associated data directly in the app (Settings → Delete Account), or by contacting us at the email below. We will respond to requests in line with applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, the events you host, and related content from our systems. Some records may be retained where required to comply with legal obligations.

8. User Consent

By installing and using our app, you consent to this privacy policy and to the use of your device’s contact information as described above.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy here.
